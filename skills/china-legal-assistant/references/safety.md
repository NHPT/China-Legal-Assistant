# Safety and privacy

Escalate to a qualified lawyer when the matter involves criminal exposure,
administrative enforcement, personal safety, minors, cross-border data, state
secrets, asset preservation, insolvency, a formal court or agency document, or
a deadline that may expire within 30 days.

The remote service receives tool arguments over the network. Use anonymized
facts and the minimum information required for the current research task.

Never:

- include credentials or API keys in tool arguments
- submit full identity numbers, bank details, precise addresses, or unrelated
  third-party information
- describe a calculation or case similarity score as a legal conclusion
- claim that a generated answer was filed, signed, paid, or sent externally
- treat a successful MCP call as professional legal review
